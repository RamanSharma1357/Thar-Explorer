Privacy Policy

Effective Date: August 15, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Thar Explorer ("the App") handles information when you use our educational game.

Thar Explorer is an educational game designed to provide simple and enjoyable learning activities involving letters, numbers, counting, and basic mathematics.

1. Information We Collect

Thar Explorer itself does not ask users to create an account and does not directly request personal information such as name, email address, phone number, home address, or password.

The game does not provide a user registration system or require users to submit personal information in order to play.

2. Advertising and Google AdMob

Thar Explorer may display advertisements using Google Mobile Ads (AdMob).

Google Mobile Ads SDK may automatically collect or share certain information for advertising, analytics, fraud prevention, and service operation. Depending on the configuration and applicable policies, this may include:

  • IP address
  • Device or advertising identifiers
  • App and user interaction information
  • Advertising information
  • Diagnostic and performance information
  • Information about ad views and interactions

This information is handled by Google according to Google's applicable privacy policies and advertising requirements.

We do not directly receive or control all information that may be collected automatically by the Google Mobile Ads SDK.

3. Rewarded Advertisements

Thar Explorer may offer optional rewarded advertisements. A user may choose to watch an advertisement in order to receive an in-game reward.

Watching an advertisement is not required to use the basic learning features of the game.

4. Children's Privacy

Thar Explorer is designed as a child-friendly educational game.

We do not knowingly request children to provide personal information such as their name, email address, telephone number, physical address, or password.

Where advertising is provided to children or users of unknown age, advertising and data practices are intended to follow applicable platform and advertising requirements, including applicable Google Play Families requirements.

We do not knowingly use Thar Explorer to collect personal information directly from children.

5. Third-Party Services

The App may use third-party services that operate independently from us.

Google Mobile Ads / AdMob

Google Mobile Ads may process information for advertising, analytics, fraud prevention, and related purposes.

Users should review Google's privacy documentation for additional information about how Google handles information through its advertising services.

6. Internet Connection

Some features, including advertisements, may require an internet connection.

The availability of advertisements may depend on the user's internet connection, device, location, advertising inventory, and other factors.

7. Data Security

We take reasonable steps to avoid unnecessary collection of personal information through the App.

Information processed by third-party services may be protected using security measures provided by those services.
