Privacy Policy — Thar Explorer

Effective date: August 15, 2026

Thar Explorer is an educational game designed for children and families. This privacy policy explains how information is handled when you use the game.

1. Information We Collect

Thar Explorer does not require an account and does not intentionally collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, precise location, contacts, photos, or other personal information from players.

2. Game Data

Game questions, rounds and scores are generated and handled locally while the game is running. The game does not provide a user account system or a server-side profile.

3. Third-Party Services

The web version loads React and Babel libraries from third-party CDNs and may load Google Fonts. Those services may process technical information according to their own policies. If this game is packaged into another app or platform that adds advertising, analytics, or other services, the privacy practices of those services will also apply.

4. Children's Privacy

The game is intended to be family-friendly. We do not knowingly request or collect personal information from children.

5. Security

Because the game does not maintain a user account or personal profile, there is no personal account database maintained by the game.

6. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated when the game or its services change. The effective date above will be updated when material changes are made.
